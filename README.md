# MicroPad-Pro
<img width="660" height="347" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-25 191138" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f8fef3eb-6745-4a0b-a758-8b7379f2bb74" />

So I made a custom 3X3 micropad with three rotor encoder that controls Brightness, Volume , scroll . The Micropad is based on Arduino Pro-Micro and each switch has a diode connected to it to prevent ghosting. The micropad is mainly designed for Normal day short Cuts . PCB was designed on kicad and 3D model was made in fusion 360
### Features
- Based on Arduino Pro-Micro
- has a compact design
- has diode to protect ghosting
- 3x3 Switch Matrix and 3 Rotor to control Volume ,  Brightness ,scroll
